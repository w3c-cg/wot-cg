## Introduction

This past year, the Web of Things took meaningful steps forward thanks to our diverse community of contributors. We'd like to share a few of the highlights with you.

## Working Group Section

Thanks to the working group, new features have arrived in the Web of Things standard. The Thing Description gained Common Definitions, which let you declare shared details once instead of repeating them across a description, starting with form defaults. We added a binding registry and new protocol bindings to reach more real-world systems, and data-mapping features are on the way. There's also a new charter, setting the direction for the years ahead - so if you're interested in standardization for the Internet of Things, this is a good moment to get involved.

## Community Group Section

The community group held six meetups and expanded our tutorial library, where you can get hands-on with the Web of Things yourself. This year, the first specification of the Web Thing Protocol was released. It's a WebSocket sub-protocol, built natively on the Thing Description model, that lets an application talk to one or more connected devices in real time. Implementations are already running, and it's heading toward the Working Group and a standards track.
