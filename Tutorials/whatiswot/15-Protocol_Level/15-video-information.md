## Title

Forms and Bindings | What is WoT

## Description

In this video, we look at how Interaction Affordances actually communicate over the network through Bindings and Forms. A Form maps an operation, such as reading a property or invoking an action, to a concrete protocol message, telling the Consumer which protocol to use, where to send the request, and how the data is encoded. The corresponding web page of this video is at https://w3c.github.io/wot-cg/tutorials/whatiswot/docs/wot/thing-description/forms-and-bindings .
